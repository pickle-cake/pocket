Hear where every stroke lands

Pocket is a timing coach for your practice pad. It plays a click, listens to each stroke through the microphone and shows how early or late it landed, in milliseconds.

Start practisingSee how it works

Free, with no ads and no account. It runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set up once

    The welcome runs a mic check: stay quiet for two seconds, then hit the pad twelve times. Auto calibrate then times how late sound reaches your device, so the numbers are honest.

  2. 2

    Pick an exercise

    Start with the built-in starter routines or the standard rudiments, or write your own sticking, such as RLRR LRLL. Put several exercises together and they play as a routine, in order.

  3. 3

    Play on the line

    Choose a tempo and press Start. After the count-in, a line moves across the notation and reaches each note exactly when it is due. Strike as it arrives.

  4. 4

    Read the result

    Every stroke is coloured on the staff with how early or late it was. You get your share on time, your average lean, how even your strokes were, and the weak spots to practise next.

You need a practice pad, sticks and a quiet room for the set-up. Once you play, headphones keep the click out of the microphone. No microphone? A keyboard works as a rougher test mode.

How it helps you practise

A metronome tells you where the beat is. It cannot tell you where you were. Pocket listens, so you find out what your hands actually do, and what to work on next.

  • Know your tendency

    A gauge shows whether you lean early or late over the last 32 strokes. Many drummers rush without hearing it. Now you can see it.

  • Hear the weak hand

    Level bars show each stroke's loudness against your own typical stroke, with left and right apart, so an uneven hand stands out.

  • Build your inner clock

    Four click levels thin the click from every stroke to beat one of each bar. Master clicks only the count-in, so you hold the tempo yourself.

  • Speed up when you are ready

    An exercise is cleared at a tempo when 90% of its strokes are on time. Pocket names the weak exercises and suggests staying put until they settle.

  • Accents, ghost notes and graces

    Lay accents and ghost notes over any exercise and Pocket checks that you played them louder and softer. Flams and drags are scored on the main stroke.

  • Keep coming back

    Scores, medals from Hickory to Diamond, practice days and a streak that forgives one rest day a week. History keeps your last 300 runs, ready to play again.

About Pocket

Pocket is free, with no ads and no account. It is a single web page: open it in a browser on your phone or computer, and add it to your Home Screen to keep it handy. Once it has been opened, it keeps working offline.

Nothing is uploaded. Your records stay in your browser, and you can export them to keep a copy or move them to another device.

It is open source under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later. You may use, change and share it, and anything built from it must be shared the same way, with its source. The code was written with AI, which learned from the work of countless people who shared theirs freely. It is shared in that spirit.
